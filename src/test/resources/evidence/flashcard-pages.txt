=== Photosynthesis ===
Photosynthesis is how green plants make food. Chlorophyll in the chloroplasts
absorbs sunlight. The plant takes in carbon dioxide through stomata and water
through the roots. Using light energy, it converts these into glucose and
oxygen. The word equation: carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen (in the
presence of light and chlorophyll). Glucose is used for energy or stored as
starch. Oxygen is released as a by-product.

=== The Water Cycle ===
The water cycle moves water around Earth. Evaporation turns liquid water into
vapour using heat from the Sun. Condensation forms clouds as vapour cools.
Precipitation is rain, snow, or hail falling from clouds. Collection gathers
water in rivers, lakes, and oceans, where it evaporates again. Transpiration is
water vapour released from plant leaves.

=== Fractions ===
A fraction shows part of a whole. The numerator (top) is how many parts you
have; the denominator (bottom) is the total equal parts. 3/4 means three of four
equal parts. Equivalent fractions have the same value: 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8. To add
fractions with the same denominator, add the numerators and keep the
denominator. To compare, use a common denominator.

=== Forces and Motion ===
A force is a push or a pull, measured in newtons (N). Balanced forces cancel out
and an object stays still or moves at constant speed. Unbalanced forces change
speed or direction. Friction is a force that opposes motion between surfaces.
Gravity pulls objects toward Earth. Newton's first law: an object stays at rest
or in uniform motion unless acted on by a resultant force.

=== The Roman Empire ===
The Roman Empire began in 27 BC when Augustus became the first emperor. It grew
around the Mediterranean Sea. Romans built roads, aqueducts to carry water, and
public baths. The army was organised into legions. Latin was the official
language. The empire split into West and East; the Western Empire fell in AD 476.
